About Us

Who We Are

Travelista started with a simple frustration: booking a trip meant juggling five different apps for flights, trains, buses and hotels — and trusting none of them completely. We set out to fix that by building a single platform where every step of a journey, from the first search to the final check-in, is fast, transparent and secure.

What began as a small team comparing flight prices for friends has grown into a platform used by travelers and verified sellers across dozens of cities. The tools changed; the goal never did — make travel planning something people can actually enjoy.

Our Mission

Why We Exist

To make booking any form of travel — flight, train, bus or stay — simple, transparent and trustworthy for everyone, regardless of how experienced a traveler they are.

Our Vision

Where We're Headed

In the next decade, we want Travelista to be the first place travelers in the region open when planning any trip — known as much for reliability as for choice.
